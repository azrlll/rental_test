LEGAL & CORPORATE GOVERNANCE ARTICLE I

1. Legal & Compliance Policy

1.1 Enterprise Registration & Licensing

DriveSphere operates under full registration with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) for private tourist and commercial fleet leasing. All motorized commercial units hold valid franchise certifications, CPC permits, and comply with DOTr national emissions standards.

1.2 Data Privacy Act Compliance (RA 10173)

Customer telemetry data, GPS tracking, and identification credentials are encrypted using AES-256 and processed solely for vehicle security, trip verification, and billing reconciliation. DriveSphere enforces strict non-disclosure policies and will never distribute, sell, or monetize information assets to unverified third parties.

National Privacy Commission (NPC) Certified Protocols

1.3 Tax Compliance & Invoicing

All vehicle hire rates include 12% Value-Added Tax (VAT) with Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) compliant Official Receipts issued electronically. Enterprise corporate clients may request creditable withholding tax certificates (BIR Form 2307) during digital settlement and monthly invoicing reconciliation.

RENTER RIGHTS & OBLIGATIONS ARTICLE II

2. Customer-Facing Rental Policies

2.1 Eligibility & Documentation

Primary drivers must be at least 21 years of age and hold an unexpired Professional or Non-Professional driver's license with at least 1 year of driving validity. Two valid government IDs and a major credit card or digital e-wallet are mandatory upon vehicle dispatch.

2.2 Fuel, Mileage & Return

Rentals are dispatched with a 100% full tank and must be returned full, or fuel replacement surcharges applies at prevailing pump rates plus a 15% refueling fee. Unlimited mileage is standard unless specified for designated promo rates.

2.3 Cancellation & Grace Period

Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to booking commencement receive a 100% refund. Cancellations under 24 hours are subject to a 1-day rental charge. A strict 30-minute grace period applies to scheduled vehicle returns prior to billing additional hourly intervals.

2.4 Deposit & Toll Settlements

Security deposits are pre-authorized upon vehicle release and returned within 3-5 business days upon vehicle inspection verifying zero damage and settling electronic tollways (RFID / Autosweep / Easytrip) deducted at exact usage receipts.

FLEET OPERATIONS & MAINTENANCE ARTICLE III

3. Internal Fleet & Operational Policies

3.1 40-Point Pre-Trip Mechanical Audit

Mandatory Check

Prior to customer handover, certified fleet technicians must sign off on computerized brake fluid, tire pressure, in-cabin tread depth, fluid levels, and diagnostic error codes. Inspection logs are digitized and paired directly to the customer's reservation ticket for transparency.

3.2 Vehicle Retirement & Age Caps

Passenger cars and SUVs are strictly retired or transitioned out of primary rental service at 36 months of service or 80,000 km, whichever arrives first. This guarantees every customer drives a modern, fuel-efficient vehicle equipped with cutting-edge active collision safety features.

3.3 Maintenance Logging & Telemetry

Automatic telemetry logs oil lifespan, engine temperatures, and emergency impact detection continuously to our central operations center in BGC. Any critical vehicle alert triggers immediate remote dispatch assessment and dispatch of roadside service units.

ROADWAY SAFETY & EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS ARTICLE IV

4. Safety & Regulatory Compliance Policies

4.1 Speed Monitoring & Geofencing

For passenger and asset safety, speeds exceeding 120 km/h on expressways or travel outside approved island boundaries trigger real-time dispatch safety advisory alerts. Renter is notified via SMS/Mobile App to regulate velocity within lawful Philippine speed restrictions.

4.2 Comprehensive Insurance & CDW Guidelines

Mandatory basic collision damage coverage applies to all trips. Willful negligence, off-roading with non-4x4 vehicles, unauthorized sub-leasing, or driving under intoxication immediately voids insurance coverage, placing full mechanical liability upon the renter.

4.3 Severe Weather & Typhoons Protocol

DriveSphere reserves the right to recall or reschedule vehicle departures during PAGASA Signal No. 3 or higher typhoons to guarantee customer safety. In such force majeure instances, booking dates are re-schedulable without penalties, or credited with a 100% voucher balance.
